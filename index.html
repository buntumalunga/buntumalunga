about

cooking • nutrition • health

established in 2022, brings together biochemistry, culinary arts training and nutrition science to explore the intersection of food, culture [world cuisines], and health, meal prep services, pop-ups, and nutrition-focused teaching experiences.

it is a space seeking to create dishes from fine dining to street food and experiment with flavours using knowledge from world cuisines and understand the process of cooking through a scientific lens with nutrition being the cornerstone.

with the increase in the prevalence of chronic diseases such as diabetes, hypertension and cardiovascular diseases, we aim to bridge nutrition science with hands-on cooking practice so that people not only know what to eat but also how to prepare it in a way that fits their lifestyle and make it enjoyable.
